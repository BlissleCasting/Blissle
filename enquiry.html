Enquiry, Booking & Casting Process

We want your journey with Blissle to be simple, smooth, and enjoyable. Here’s how it works:

  1. Explore Our Styles
    Take a moment to browse through our different styles and designs. This will help you decide what best suits your taste and requirements.
  2. Share Your Details
    Once you’ve made your choice, simply email or WhatsApp us the following information:
    • Your Name
    • Your Area/Location
    • Baby’s Age (or mention if it’s for an adult)
    • Email and Mobile Number
  3. Receive a Quick Response
    Our team will get back to you within 24–48 hours with all the details you need. Based on our response, you can go ahead and book your appointment.
  4. Secure Your Appointment
    Once your booking is confirmed, we will guide you through the payment process. After payment, your appointment will be locked in.
  5. Home Visit & Cast Capture
    On the scheduled day, a Blissle expert will visit your location to capture the casts. During this session, we will also finalize details such as frame style, colors, baby details, and preferred photograph.
  6. Cast Review
    Within 24–48 hours after capturing, we will promptly update you on the results of your casts. If the casts do not meet our quality standards, we will recast them at no additional cost.
  7. Frame Preparation
    Once all design and personalization details are finalized, our team will carefully craft your frame. This process takes about 3–4 weeks as we ensure the highest quality finish.
  8. Collection
    As soon as your frame is ready, we’ll notify you. You can collect your finished keepsake and cherish it forever.
  9. Review/Feedback/Reference
    Thank you for choosing our services. We truly value your opinion and would love to hear about your experience with us. If you have a moment, please consider leaving a review or sharing your feedback—it helps us improve and continue providing you with the highest quality service.

    If you have any suggestions or specific requests, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Your satisfaction is our top priority, and we’re always here to assist you.

    10. Note: We truly thank you for trusting Blissle to be a part of your special memories. Each keepsake tells a story, and we’re honored to help preserve yours. Your trust means the world to us, and we promise to treat every detail with the care it deserves. If at any moment you have questions or need support, know that we are always here for you. Thank you for letting us be a part of your journey—your faith in us inspires everything we do.
